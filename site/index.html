Trading in under 60 seconds

No account, no download, no jargon. Six steps from "what's a stock?" to your first report card.

  1. 01

    Open Dip Lab

    Tap Play. It runs in your browser, so there's nothing to install and nobody to sign up with. Your progress stays on your device.

    Tip: the app walks you through your first trade the first time you open it.

  2. 02

    Pick your run

    Choose a market and your starting cash. First steps is a gentle three-month warm-up. Feeling brave? Try the COVID crash. Skull icons mark the boss runs.

    Tip: start with $10,000 and $0 fees. You can change both later.

  3. 03

    Find a company you know

    The Market tab lists 550+ well-known US companies and funds. Search for one you use every day (Apple, Nike, Netflix), or browse by sector and top movers. Star it to watch it.

    Tip: SPY is a fund that holds the 500 biggest US companies at once. It's the benchmark you're racing.

  4. 04

    Buy in plain English

    Tap Buy, pick an amount, and read the preview: "Buy $500 of AAPL". A risk check tells you how much of your account the order puts on the line before you confirm.

    Tip: every order type has an "Explain like I'm new" note. Start with a market order.

  5. 05

    Press play and watch

    The market clock starts ticking. Speed it up (1x to 8x), pause any time, or jump to the next earnings report. Your portfolio shows you against SPY, day by day.

    Tip: the game pauses at each close so you can take a breath and read the day's summary.

  6. 06

    Get graded, then replay

    At the end you get an honest debrief: did you beat simply holding SPY? You're graded A to F on return, risk and discipline. Then hit Replay and face the very same market again.

    Tip: the debrief flags the moments worth learning from. Jump straight back to one and try again.

// questions

Good to know

Is this real market data?

No. Every price comes from a simulation engine. Historical scenarios are shaped to follow the rough path of the real period, but individual stock prices, earnings results and news are simulated.

Is this investment advice?

No. Dip Lab is a learning game. It teaches how markets and orders behave, not what to buy.

Do I need to know anything about stocks?

Not at all. A guided first trade, plain-English orders and glossary cards on every term mean you learn while you play. Start with the First steps scenario.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account, ever. You need a connection only for the first visit; after that the whole app, including all stock data, is stored on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, in your browser's storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Settings lets you export a run or back up everything to a file and import it on another device.

Why does replay give me exactly the same market?

Every run is built from a seed. The same seed always produces the same prices, headlines and earnings results, so you can compare strategies fairly or send a seed to a friend and race on the same market.

Does it cost anything?

No. Dip Lab is free and open-source software (GPL-3.0-or-later). No ads, no in-app purchases.
